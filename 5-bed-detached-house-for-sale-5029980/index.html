About the property

Description property

An exquisite 5 bedroom residence of unlimited potential in one of Ballina's prime residential areas.

Standing on a large elevated site with an abundance of trees & shrubs offering complete privacy to the residence.

Designed & decorated to a standard of excellence; complimented by top-class carpets & flooring; leaving nothing to do but walk in...

One of the best Family Homes to come to the market in 2026.

The accommodation extends to c. 2,580 sq ft (c.240 sq mts) and boast many fine features including generous reception rooms, well proportioned bedrooms to include 4 en-suites.

Only minutes away from this substantial home is the Quay Village, Quay National School & Ballina Rugby Club.

The property was extended to the rear some years ago and also benefits from a large detached garage, providing considerable versatility for storage, workshop space, hobbies or a variety of other uses, subject to the necessary requirements.

The property is approached via a secured gated entrance, with block-built boundary walls and a generous tarmac driveway providing ample off-street parking.

Location:-

The location is only one of the particular features of this property. The renowned River Moy and the popular Quay area are just a short walk away, with the Quay Village offering a selection of amenities including bars, restaurants and leisure facilities. The highly regarded Ice House Hotel, Keane's Bar & Shop, Quay National School and Ballina Rugby Club are also conveniently located nearby only a short walk away.

Ballina town centre is approximately one mile away, providing an extensive range of shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, sporting facilities and essential amenities. The surrounding area is renowned for its excellent recreational opportunities, including salmon fishing, golfing, shooting, boating and walking.

Accommodation & Potential Internally, the property offers exceptionally spacious accommodation, living rooms, while externally the generous grounds provide an excellent sense of privacy and space rarely found so close to town.

The combination of its substantial floor area, spacious bedrooms, large site, mature gardens, extensive parking and additional garage accommodation makes this an exciting proposition for a family seeking a spacious home in a highly convenient location.

Properties offering this level of space, grounds and proximity to Ballina town centre are seldom available to the market.

Early viewing is strongly recommended to fully appreciate the location, setting, the grounds & elegance of this impressive property.
